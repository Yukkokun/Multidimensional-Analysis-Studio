The tenant must give written notice to the landlord before the end of the period specified in clause four.
If the landlord does not respond within fourteen days, the tenant may terminate the agreement without further obligation.
Although the agreement does not require it, the parties should keep a record of any correspondence relating to the premises.
The notice that the tenant serves must be signed and dated, and it must state the address to which any reply should be sent.
Any dispute arising from the interpretation of these provisions shall be referred to arbitration.
The obligations to which the parties are subject are set out in the schedule referred to above.
Neither party nor any representative of either party may vary the terms of the agreement orally.
Because the agreement was drafted in general terms, the meaning of several clauses remains uncertain.
