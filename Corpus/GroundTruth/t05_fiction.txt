She had been waiting at the station for nearly an hour when the train finally arrived.
The platform was almost empty, and the rain had been falling steadily since the morning.
Walking slowly towards the exit, she noticed a man standing under the clock, reading a newspaper.
He looked up as she passed, and for a moment neither of them spoke.
I thought you were not coming, he said quietly.
She smiled, and they walked together through the narrow streets towards the river.
The lights along the embankment were reflected in the water, and the air smelled of diesel and wet stone.
Having finished his paper, he folded it carefully and put it in his coat pocket.
They stopped at a small cafe near the bridge, where they sat for a long time without saying anything.
Later, when the rain had stopped, they crossed the bridge and disappeared into the crowd.
