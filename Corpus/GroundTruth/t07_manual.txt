Before you begin, make sure that the device is disconnected from the mains supply.
Remove the cover by sliding it gently towards you, and set it aside in a safe place.
You should not attempt to open the housing unless the indicator light has gone out completely.
To really improve performance, clean the filter every two weeks.
If the filter is badly clogged, you may need to replace it rather than wash it.
Do not use solvents, because they can damage the surface of the housing.
Having replaced the filter, press the reset button and wait for the light to stop flashing.
You must check the seal before you reconnect the device.
Replacing a worn seal is usually straightforward, although it requires some patience.
