The film is remarkable, and it is easily the best thing the director has made.
The performances are extremely convincing, particularly in the quieter scenes.
Some viewers may find the pacing somewhat slow, but the patience is rewarded.
There is no convincing reason to avoid this film, and there is every reason to see it.
It seems that the director has finally found a subject worthy of his talents.
The ending is perhaps a little too neat, and the final speech is almost embarrassingly sincere.
I really did not expect to be moved by it, and yet I was.
Sort of a quiet triumph, and one that deserves a much wider audience.
