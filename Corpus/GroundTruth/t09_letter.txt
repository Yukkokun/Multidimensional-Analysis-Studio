Dear Margaret,
Thank you for your letter, which arrived on Friday morning.
I am sorry that I have not written sooner, but the last few weeks have been unusually busy.
We have finally finished the work on the back room, and it looks much better than I expected.
You would not recognise the garden now, because we planted the hedge that you suggested.
I hope you and Peter are well, and that the weather has improved since we last spoke.
Please let me know whether you can come in June, because we would love to see you both.
I will write again when I have more news.
With love,
Eleanor
