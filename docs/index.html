01 — Overview

First: what corpus is this?

Words, documents, six dimension scores, and text types ranked by distance.

  • Single-document mode
  • Each dimension states its pole

02 — Dimension

Where it lands among the genres

Eight reference genres and their ranges, with your corpus overlaid.

  • Overlay a second corpus
  • Sortable reference table

03 — Text type

Does the profile fit?

Eight reference profiles behind your corpus mean, per-document curves as context.

  • Per-document lines fade out

04 — Feature

Which variable actually moved

Frequency and z for all 67 features, filterable by dimension.

  • Extremes are flagged

05 — Compare

Two corpora, one verdict

Welch's t and Mann–Whitney U per feature, plus BH-FDR or Holm.

  • Normality before the test

Fidelity

Pretty charts are worthless if the numbers drift

Ported rule by rule from MAT's Perl sources, checked byte for byte. The original's bugs are kept too.

Rounding follows the original

Frequencies and z rounded, dimension sums not.

Quirks preserved

Short-text TTR +1, INPR shadowed by QUPR.

You can verify it

Golden corpora and scripts are in the repo.
